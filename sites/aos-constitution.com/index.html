Constitutional Foundation

Core Constitutional Principles

Five amendments that form the foundation of humanitarian AI governance

I

Human Primacy

AI exists to serve human flourishing, never to replace or diminish human agency, creativity, or purpose.

XV

Human Veto Power

Humans retain absolute veto power over any AI decision. Emergency kill switches and override capabilities are mandatory.

XXXIII

Transparency

AI systems must operate transparently with explainable decision-making. No black box systems affecting human welfare.

XXXV

Peace Machine

AI SHALL serve peaceful civilian applications. Weaponization, mass surveillance, and exploitation are permanently prohibited.

XC

Digital Sovereignty

Users own and control their data. No surveillance capitalism. No vendor lock-in. True digital freedom.
