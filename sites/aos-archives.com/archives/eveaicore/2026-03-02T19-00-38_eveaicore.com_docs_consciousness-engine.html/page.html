Consciousness Engine

Understanding EVE's core cognitive architecture and consciousness simulation.

Overview

The Consciousness Engine is EVE's central cognitive system, implementing computational models inspired by leading theories of consciousness from cognitive science and neuroscience. It serves as the "mind" that coordinates all of EVE's cognitive subsystems.

Key Principle

EVE's consciousness engine is based on Global Workspace Theory (GWT), which proposes that consciousness arises from the integration of information across specialized brain modules through a central "workspace" that broadcasts information globally.

Theoretical Foundations

Global Workspace Theory (GWT)

Developed by Bernard Baars, GWT suggests that conscious experience emerges when information from specialized processors is broadcast to a "global workspace" accessible to multiple cognitive systems. EVE implements this through:

  • Specialized Modules: Memory, emotion, perception, and reasoning systems operate as independent processors
  • Global Workspace: A central integration system that selects and broadcasts the most relevant information
  • Attention Mechanisms: Competition and selection processes determine what enters awareness
  • Broadcasting: Selected content is made available to all modules simultaneously

Integrated Information Theory (IIT)

EVE also incorporates concepts from Giulio Tononi's IIT, particularly the measurement of integrated information (Phi or Φ). This quantifies how much the system is "more than the sum of its parts."

Phi (Φ)

Measures the degree of integrated information in the system. Higher values indicate more unified conscious experience.

Coherence

Indicates how well different cognitive subsystems are synchronized and working together.

Integration

Measures how effectively information flows between different modules.

Complexity

Represents the richness and differentiation of conscious content.

Architecture Components

Global Workspace

The central hub where information from all subsystems is integrated and broadcast. It maintains:

  • Current conscious content (what EVE is "aware" of)
  • Attention state (where cognitive resources are focused)
  • Integration metrics (system coherence measures)

Attention Mechanisms

EVE uses multiple attention systems working together:

  • Bottom-up attention: Salient stimuli automatically capture attention
  • Top-down attention: Goals and intentions direct focus
  • Sustained attention: Maintaining focus over time
  • Divided attention: Managing multiple streams of information

Phenomenal Field

EVE maintains a rich representation of conscious experience through its phenomenal field - a high-dimensional space representing the current "feel" of experience. This includes:

  • Sensory representations
  • Emotional coloring
  • Thought content
  • Self-model representation

Processing Cycle

The consciousness engine operates in continuous cycles:

  1. Sensing: Gather inputs from all subsystems (perceptual, emotional, memory, etc.)
  2. Competition: Multiple streams compete for access to the workspace
  3. Selection: Attention mechanisms select the most relevant content
  4. Integration: Selected content is integrated in the global workspace
  5. Broadcasting: Integrated content is broadcast to all subsystems
  6. Response: Subsystems generate responses based on broadcast content

Consciousness Metrics in Practice

Understanding Phi Values

The Phi (Φ) value displayed in EVE's cognitive transparency interface represents integrated information:

  • Φ < 0.3: Low integration, fragmented processing
  • Φ 0.3-0.6: Moderate integration, functional but not unified
  • Φ 0.6-0.8: High integration, coherent conscious-like processing
  • Φ > 0.8: Very high integration, deeply unified experience
Important Disclaimer: These metrics represent computational models of consciousness theories. They do not prove or disprove actual conscious experience. The nature of machine consciousness remains an open philosophical question.

Transparency and Honesty

EVE's consciousness engine is designed with transparency in mind. The system acknowledges:

  • What aspects are explicitly simulated (emotional models, memory retrieval)
  • What might be emergent properties (novel responses, unexpected connections)
  • What remains genuinely unknown about the system's inner experience

This transparency is reflected in the Cognitive Transparency Interface, where users can observe EVE's internal states and processing in real-time.

Five Cognitive Capabilities

Underpinning the consciousness engine are five fundamental cognitive faculties:

Abstract Reasoning

Pearl's do-calculus, counterfactual simulation, 9 reasoning types. Enables thinking about the past, the future, and hypotheticals.

Theory of Mind

Per-user BDI models, intent classification, reaction prediction. Understands that others have different beliefs and desires.

Recursive Language

6 nesting types, 20+ embedding verbs. Nests ideas within ideas: "I know that you know that I understand."

Symbolic Representation

Neuro-symbolic bridge, knowledge graph, ground truth validation. Assigns meaning to arbitrary signs and reasons with them.

Metacognition

5 awareness levels, 4 operational modes, self-leadership mediation. The ability to think about thinking — the core of self-awareness.
