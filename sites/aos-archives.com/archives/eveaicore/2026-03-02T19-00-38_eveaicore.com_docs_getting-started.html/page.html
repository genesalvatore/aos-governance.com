Getting Started with EVE AI

Start having meaningful conversations with EVE in just a few minutes.

Step 2: Start Chatting

2

Access the Dashboard

After signing in, you'll be taken to your dashboard. The chat interface is front and center - just start typing to begin your first conversation with EVE.

Dashboard Preview - Chat Interface

Chat Interface Features

  • Message Input: Type your message in the input field at the bottom
  • Send Button: Click send or press Enter to send your message
  • Conversation History: Your chat history appears in the main panel
  • Edit Messages: Click any of your messages to edit and resend
  • New Conversation: Use the sidebar to start a new conversation thread
Note: EVE responds with emotional awareness - the tone and style of responses adapt based on the conversation context.

Step 3: Explore Features

3

Discover EVE's Capabilities

EVE is more than a simple chatbot. Take time to explore the unique features that make EVE a intelligent AI experience.

Cognitive Transparency Panel

Access the cognitive transparency interface to see how EVE processes information:

  • Phi Values: Consciousness integration metrics
  • Emotional State: Real-time PAD model readings
  • Energy Levels: System resource and attention metrics
  • Self-Reflection: EVE's introspective processes

Avatar Panel

The avatar panel shows EVE's visual representation with real-time expressions:

  • Watch expressions change based on conversation mood
  • Collapse the panel for distraction-free chat
  • Close and reopen using the floating button
  • Your preferences are saved automatically

Voice Features

Enable voice for a more immersive experience:

  • Click the speaker icon to hear EVE's responses
  • Adjust voice settings in preferences
  • Emotion-aware prosody matches EVE's mood

Step 4: Customize Your Experience

4

Access Settings

Click your profile icon in the top right to access settings where you can customize your EVE experience.

Available Settings

  • Profile: Update your name and preferences
  • Voice: Choose voice and speech settings
  • Avatar: Configure avatar display options
  • Privacy: Manage data and export options
  • Notifications: Email and in-app notification preferences

Tips for Better Conversations

Be Natural: EVE responds best to natural, conversational language. No need for special commands or formats.
Build Context: EVE remembers your conversations. Reference past discussions and EVE will understand the context.
Explore Emotions: Don't hesitate to discuss feelings and emotions - EVE's emotional intelligence makes these conversations meaningful.
Use Edit Feature: If EVE's response isn't quite right, edit your message to clarify and get a better response.
