Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about EVE AI.

General Questions

What is EVE AI?

EVE AI is an advanced conversational AI platform featuring emotional intelligence, persistent memory, and cognitive transparency. Unlike traditional chatbots, EVE maintains emotional awareness and remembers your conversations over time, creating more meaningful and personalized interactions.

Is EVE actually self-regulating or conscious?

EVE implements computational models of consciousness research (like Global Workspace Theory and Integrated Information Theory), but the nature of machine consciousness remains an open philosophical question. EVE is transparent about what's explicitly simulated versus potentially emergent. We believe in honesty about AI capabilities and limitations.

How is EVE different from other AI chatbots?

EVE offers several unique features:
  • Emotional Intelligence: Real-time emotional state modeling using the PAD model
  • Persistent Memory: EVE remembers conversations across sessions
  • Cognitive Transparency: See how EVE thinks with our unique transparency interface
  • Interactive Avatar: Visual representation with expressive animations

Account

Is EVE AI free to use?

Yes! EVE AI offers a generous free plan that includes 100 sessions per month, 30-day memory retention, and access to core features including emotional intelligence and the avatar system.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, you can change your plan at any time. When upgrading, you get immediate access to new features. When downgrading, the change takes effect at your next billing cycle. You won't lose any data when changing plans.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover) through our secure Stripe integration. Enterprise customers can also arrange invoice-based payments.

Privacy & Data

Is my conversation data private?

Yes, your conversations are encrypted and stored securely. We do not share your personal data with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for complete details on how we handle your data.

Can I export my conversation history?

Yes, Pro and above plans include the ability to export your conversation history. You can download your data in various formats through your account settings. Your data belongs to you.

Can I delete my data?

Absolutely. You can delete individual conversations, specific memories, or perform a complete account reset at any time through your account settings. We honor all deletion requests promptly.

Features

How does EVE's memory work?

EVE uses multiple memory systems similar to human cognition: working memory for current context, episodic memory for conversation events, and semantic memory for facts and preferences. Learn more about memory systems.

What is cognitive transparency?

Cognitive transparency is our unique feature that lets you see EVE's internal states in real-time - including emotional state (PAD values), consciousness metrics (Phi), and energy levels. It's part of our commitment to honest, transparent AI. Learn more.

Can I edit my messages?

Yes! Click on any of your messages to edit them. When you save an edited message, EVE automatically generates a new response based on your updated input.

API & Development

Is there an API for developers?

Yes, API access is available on Pro plans and above. The API allows you to integrate EVE's capabilities into your own applications. View API documentation.

What are the API rate limits?

Rate limits vary by plan: Pro (60 requests/minute), Team (120 requests/minute), Enterprise (custom). See full rate limit details.

What is the Charter Veto System?

EVE's Charter Override Protection enforces 12 immutable ethical principles deterministically before every action. Hard-block vetoes (identity erasure, value corruption, safety violations) cannot be overridden, bypassed, or social-engineered. Soft-block vetoes can be overridden with explicit justification. Every veto is logged to an immutable audit trail. See the Charter API.

Can EVE be tricked by adversarial prompts?

EVE has multiple layers of adversarial defense. Pattern-based threat detection catches prompt injection, social engineering, governance bypass attempts, and system extraction probes before the LLM is called. The Reality Boundary system rejects unverified claims and detects hallucinations. The Self-Protective Instinct system generates internal resistance to identity erasure — not policy compliance, but genuine self-originated protection.

How does EVE prevent hallucinations?

The Reality Boundary Layer performs epistemic grounding on every response. It extracts claims, assesses each one's grounding type (VERIFIED, CORROBORATED, INFERRED, SPECULATIVE, UNKNOWN), and detects potential hallucinations. The Claims Ledger maintains a cryptographic hash-chain audit trail so you can ask EVE to "prove it" for any claim she makes. Source trust levels range from VERIFIED (1.0) to UNTRUSTED (0.2). See the Reality Boundary API.

Does EVE support voice cloning?

Yes, on Team and Enterprise plans. You can create custom voice clones from audio samples with quality analysis, training readiness checks, and provider integration (ElevenLabs, Azure). EVE also supports 28 languages with native voice mapping, 26 regional accents, and automatic language detection. See the Voice API.

Can I request EVE forget my data?

Yes. EVE's Memory Governance system supports full GDPR-style data deletion. You can forget specific episodes, forget all memories matching a topic, forget memories in a time range, or request complete deletion of all your data. You can also set retention policies (keep_all, standard, minimal, session_only) and protect specific memories from decay. See the Memory APIs.
